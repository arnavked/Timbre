Timbre — headphone and earphone EQ workbench

Drag a dot, or focus the graph and use the arrow keys

Use the arrow keys to move the selected band. Up and down change gain, left and right change frequency; hold Shift for finer steps. Left and right bracket change Q. Delete disables the selected band. Every control is also available as a number field in the Bands tab.
